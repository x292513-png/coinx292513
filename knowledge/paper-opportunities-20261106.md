# Continuous opportunity review, independent of notification cadence

Observed production failure: five registered plans, five cancellations, one armed event and zero entries. ETH plan 10 confirmed at 2026-11-06 09:34:59 KST above 2243.42, but required a return to 2224.75 and expired at 12:00:59. In twice-daily notification mode, the engine's generic structural AI triggers were disabled. Paper execution continuously watched old registered orders; it did not continuously choose new trades.

The replacement is a broad opportunity-review trigger, not a standalone RSI strategy. On new closed five-minute bars, existing live snapshots are evaluated for 5m/15m/1h displacement, recent confirmed wave structures, confirmed divergences, confirmed swing breakouts, and directional reactions at channels, Fibonacci, moving averages and ICT references. No individual signal family is mandatory. Measurements nominate a review; the existing full AI pipeline uses top-down frames, book rules/reading protocol, historical/fractal context and actual prices to choose long, short or wait. This is not a full autonomous NEoWave parser or continuous LLM inference.

Reviews have their own `paper` call category, capped initially at 12 attempts per KST day across symbols (including failed attempts), with a 15-minute per-symbol review interval. Unchanged references are suppressed for one hour. Pending reviews reserve capacity. Manual queries retain priority. Additional reviews consume Codex account usage, but scanning alone does not call a model. Regular 09:00/21:00 notifications remain unchanged; opportunity analyses are quiet and their plans/results appear in existing paper reports and queries.

Live positions prevent duplicate-entry reviews. Waiting/armed unfilled orders do not block new opportunity assessment. New analyses use existing registration/replacement, actual next-open market simulation, structural stop, 1.5R first target minimum for market entries, shared capital/risk and forward-only fills. No old trades or profits are fabricated. Stale prices, recent candle gaps, pauses and duplicate bars prevent review requests. A delayed AI result still passes the existing publication freshness/invalidation gate before paper registration.

`opportunity_status:<symbol>` persists candidates, last scan time, job ID and a concrete reason (no candidate, pending analysis, open position, cooldown, daily budget). `/status` exposes the separate call counter; detailed paper reports show scan reasons. New prompt instructions require comparison of immediate/next-open entries with conditional retracement, without forcing either method or fabricating favorable targets.
